# Files

- [Accounts & Holdings Views (read path)](accounts-and-holdings-views.md) - The holdings read path end to end — the accounts dashboard and account detail routes, the cross-account /holdings page, the position-service aggregation and pricing-completeness rules that feed totals, holdings-table columns, grouping and preferences, the client-side paging and account-totals waves that fill the rows, and the per-currency header aggregation.
- [AI Analysis Flows](ai-analysis.md) - How retail-portfolio wires AI analysis — context assembly from the security, price and note repositories, the fundamentals / summarize-notes / portfolio-debate endpoints and their timeout and error mapping, the asynchronous note-title Huey task with its never-fail fallback, model and key configuration, and stub selection in tests.
- [Broker Connect, Import & Position Sync](broker-sync.md) - The end-to-end broker flow in retail-portfolio — listing integration-enabled institutions, logging in to Wealthsimple with credential/OTP handling and keyring session caching, importing broker users/accounts/positions, the Huey sync task that resolves broker symbols into market securities, replaces positions per account and refreshes free cash, broker value and last_sync_at, the Redis active-sync bookkeeping and WebSocket events, the frontend accounts list that consumes them, and the error-to-message mapping plus failure email.
- [CSV Account Import & Sync](csv-import.md) - The institution-configured CSV flow — how an Institution.csv_format positional template plus header aliases turns a broker export into discovered accounts, the parser's validation error taxonomy, cash-to-free-cash and option filtering, the inspect / import / csv-sync lifecycle that creates or replaces accounts and positions, the user-supplied net-deposits value that is not in the CSV, security resolution, and the Wealthsimple template defined and seeded in src/commands/seed.py.
- [Market Data, Indicators & the Price Update Cascade](market-data-and-indicators.md) - How market data moves through the system — the still-EODHD read-through price repository behind the user-facing and scheduled flows, the two price-aggregation implementations and Heikin-Ashi conversion, the two indicator paths into the stateless Go sidecar with their Redis caches, and the hourly cascade of totals recalculation, alert evaluation and email dispatch that only a --periodic worker arms.
- [Portfolios & Holdings Filters](portfolios.md) - The portfolio system end to end — the `portfolios` / `portfolio_accounts` models and their Alembic revision, PortfolioService and its ownership validation, the `/portfolios` list/create/rename/delete plus account-membership sync router surface, the server-loaded `/portfolios` page with per-currency member-account totals on every card, the accounts-UI create modal, and the `/holdings?portfolio_id=` filter seam that resolves a portfolio into a set of account ids.
- [Realtime, Background Jobs & the Worker](realtime-and-background-jobs.md) - The cross-process asynchronous runtime of retail-portfolio — the Huey worker and its service registry, the periodic and on-demand tasks, the hourly price-update cascade with isolated enqueues and retries, the Redis key and channel inventory, the Redis pub/sub WebSocket fan-out with per-event-loop clients and ticket auth, the account-sync status key contract and the frontend consumer that hydrates and polls it, and the worker dashboard mounted on the backend app.
- [Security Detail Page & Actions Sidebar](security-detail-page.md) - How the /security/[security_id] route composes its non-chart surface — the shell-first server load and SecurityPageDataService data wave, the instant titlebar lookup from the default watchlist, the page-owned cross-group state and its two-way bindings, and the seven actions-sidebar groups that own notes, documents, price alerts, indicator configuration, holdings, the valuation range, institutional ownership and AI analysis, including each group's data source, per-user scoping, persistence and documented gaps.
- [Watchlists, Sidebar & Global Search](watchlists-and-search.md) - The watchlist subsystem end to end — the market_watchlists and membership tables with their position/added_at metadata, the router-only WatchlistRepository contract and its ownership, duplicate-name and exact-permutation order rules, the ten watchlist routes with their price-enriched reads, the layout-owned WatchlistService that is the single client-side owner of watchlist state, the sidebar watchlist groups and ticker shortcuts, the /watchlists page with create/rename/delete, sort modes and manual reordering, and the global-search dialog that resolves or creates securities and toggles membership.
