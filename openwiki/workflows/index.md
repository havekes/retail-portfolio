# Files

- [Accounts & Holdings Views (read path)](accounts-and-holdings-views.md) - The holdings read path end to end — the HoldingRead / UserHoldingRead / AccountHoldingsRead / AccountTotals contracts, PositionService cost-and-cash aggregation with explicit P/L basis and unpriced-position reporting, the post-navigation paging loop and per-account totals wave in HoldingsService, client-side stock grouping with mixed-currency handling, column/group preference persistence, and the tests that pin all of it.
- [AI Analysis Flows](ai-analysis.md) - How retail-portfolio wires AI analysis — context assembly from the security, price and note repositories, the fundamentals / summarize-notes / portfolio-debate endpoints and their timeout and error mapping, the asynchronous note-title Huey task with its never-fail fallback, model and key configuration, and stub selection in tests.
- [Broker Connect, Import & Position Sync](broker-sync.md) - The end-to-end broker flow in retail-portfolio — listing integration-enabled institutions, logging in to Wealthsimple with credential/OTP handling and keyring session caching, importing broker users/accounts/positions, the Huey sync task that resolves broker symbols into market securities, replaces positions per account and refreshes cash and the broker-reported account value, the Redis active-sync bookkeeping and WebSocket events, the frontend accounts list that consumes them, and the error-to-message mapping plus failure email.
- [CSV Account Import & Sync](csv-import.md) - The CSV account lifecycle — the positional Institution.csv_format template plus header aliases that turn a broker export into discovered accounts, the parser's validation and cash/option filtering, the upload → inspect → import → csv-sync state machine with its create/update branches and net-deposits handling, and the frontend import/update modals.
- [Market Data, Indicators & the Price Update Cascade](market-data-and-indicators.md) - How the app's own market data is ingested and transformed: the EODHD read-through price repository, the daily/hourly Huey jobs and the recalculate-then-alert cascade they trigger, the two indicator paths — the in-process daily/weekly endpoint and the chart's compute route to the Go sidecar — plus the user-JWT market read routes and the Redis caches behind them.
- [Portfolios & Holdings Filters](portfolios.md) - The portfolio system end to end — the `portfolios` / `portfolio_accounts` models and their Alembic revision, PortfolioService and its ownership validation, the `/portfolios` list/create/rename/delete plus account-membership sync router surface, the server-loaded `/portfolios` page with its per-currency card totals and the accounts-UI create modal, and the `/holdings?portfolio_id=` filter seam that resolves a portfolio into a set of account ids (including how an unknown or empty portfolio degrades).
- [Realtime, Background Jobs & the Worker](realtime-and-background-jobs.md) - The cross-process asynchronous runtime of retail-portfolio — the Huey worker container and its service registry, the full task inventory with periodic scheduling, retries and the hourly price-update cascade, the Redis pub/sub WebSocket fan-out with per-event-loop clients and single-use ticket auth, the Redis account-sync status keys, the frontend consumer that hydrates and polls them, and the worker dashboard mounted on the backend app.
- [Security Detail Page & Actions Sidebar](security-detail-page.md) - How the /security/[security_id] route composes its non-chart surface — the shell-first server load and the SecurityPageDataService data wave, the instant titlebar lookup from the default watchlist, the page-owned cross-group state and its two-way bindings, the seven actions-sidebar groups that own notes, documents, price alerts, indicator configuration, holdings, fundamentals/valuation with its institutional-ownership block, and AI analysis, and the market read routes each part depends on.
- [Watchlists, Sidebar & Global Search](watchlists-and-search.md) - The watchlist subsystem end to end — the market_watchlists and membership tables with their position/added_at metadata, the router-only WatchlistRepository contract and its ownership, duplicate-name and exact-permutation order rules, the ten watchlist routes with their price-enriched reads, the layout-owned WatchlistService that is the single client-side owner of watchlist state, the sidebar watchlist groups and ticker shortcuts, the /watchlists page with create/rename/delete, sort modes and manual reordering, the two symbol-search surfaces (the user-JWT /market/search the dialog uses versus the service-token data-plane symbols/search behind the MCP resolve_symbol tool), and the global-search dialog that resolves or creates securities and toggles membership.
