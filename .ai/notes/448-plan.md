## Plan

**Approach:**
Standardize drawing-level hover tracking across all 5 drawing plugins (`horizontal-line`, `free-form-line`, `measure`, `fibonacci`, `elliott-wave`) by subscribing each primitive to `lineHovered()` from `mouseHandlers` and propagating drawing-level hover to renderer items, ensuring anchor points render whenever the mouse hovers anywhere on the drawing or when selected without triggering point highlight rings (reserving highlight rings strictly for direct anchor point hover/drag). Replace Lucide Minus and Slash in `drawing-toolbar.svelte` with new dedicated, visually balanced SVG icons (`horizontal-line-icon.svelte` and `line-icon.svelte`), add interactive Undo and Redo toolbar buttons with shortcut tooltips wired to `DrawingHistoryManager` and bound to reactive `canUndo`/`canRedo` states, and implement keyboard shortcuts for opening the chart settings modal (`Cmd+,` / `Ctrl+,`) and switching its tabs (`Cmd+1..3` / `Ctrl+1..3` with `event.preventDefault()`).

- In `elliott-wave.ts`, ensure `point.isHovered` is strictly isolated to the exact wave point being hovered (`hoveredPoint.wave === pt.wave`), preventing the bug where line hover inadvertently triggered point highlight rings on all wave anchors.
